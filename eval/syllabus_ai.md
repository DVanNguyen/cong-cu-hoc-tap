## 2. Chuỗi Khái Niệm Phục Vụ Xây Dựng Đồ Thị (Knowledge Graph DAG)

Các khái niệm có quan hệ tiền đề (`prerequisite_of`) rất rõ ràng:
1. `Agent` $\rightarrow$ `Rationality` $\rightarrow$ `PEAS Specification` $\rightarrow$ `Environment Types`
2. `Problem-Solving Agent` $\rightarrow$ `Formulation (Goal & Problem)` $\rightarrow$ `State Space` $\rightarrow$ `Search Tree`
3. `Search Strategy` $\rightarrow$ `Uninformed Search (BFS, DFS, UCS)` $\rightarrow$ `Evaluation Metrics (Completeness, Time, Space, Optimality)`
4. `Informed Search` $\rightarrow$ `Heuristic Function h(n)` $\rightarrow$ `Greedy Best-First` $\rightarrow$ `$A^*$ Search` $\rightarrow$ `Admissibility & Consistency`
5. `Complex Environments` $\rightarrow$ `Local Search (Hill-Climbing, Simulated Annealing)` $\rightarrow$ `Genetic Algorithms`
